1. Introduction

Welcome to SmartAV Media Player ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information and device permissions when using our Android mobile application.

We strongly respect your privacy. Because SmartAV is a local offline multimedia player, all file scanning, audio playback, video rendering, and equalizer processing are performed strictly on your local device hardware without internet transmission.

2. Information Collection and Use

SmartAV Media Player DOES NOT collect, log, track, or share:

  • Personal Identifiable Information (such as name, email, phone number, or physical address).
  • Financial details or payment information.
  • Location data or GPS coordinates.
  • Device identifiers or advertising tracking IDs.
  • Your private videos, songs, audio recordings, or playback history.

3. Android Device Permissions & Why We Need Them

To perform its core functions as an HD video and music player, the application requests the following specific permissions:

  • READ_MEDIA_VIDEO (Android 13+): Required to scan, index, and playback local video files stored on your device. Used strictly to display your videos in the app library.
  • READ_MEDIA_AUDIO (Android 13+): Required to discover, organize, and play your local audio files, songs, albums, and playlists.
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 12 and below): Required on legacy Android versions to read local video and audio files from your shared device storage.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK (Android 14+): Required to provide continuous, uninterrupted audio and background video playback when you minimize the app or turn off the screen, accompanied by system media playback notification controls.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): Required to display the playback control notification (Play, Pause, Next, Previous, Close) in your notification shade and lock screen.
  • WAKE_LOCK: Keeps the screen active while watching fullscreen videos and prevents playback from stopping when listening to music with the screen locked.
  • MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS: Required for the 5-band Equalizer, Bass Boost, 3D Surround Virtualizer, and hardware volume management.
Google Play Compliance Note: SmartAV Media Player does NOT request or use READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, as it is strictly dedicated to video and audio playback.

4. Private PIN Vault Security

SmartAV Media Player includes a Private Vault feature that allows users to protect sensitive videos or songs behind a 4-digit PIN lock.

  • Your PIN code is stored locally on your device using Android's secure encrypted preferences.
  • Media files moved to the vault are hidden locally on your device and are never uploaded or synced to cloud servers.
  • If you uninstall the app or clear app data, please ensure you unhide your files beforehand.

5. Third-Party Services and Analytics

SmartAV Media Player does not integrate third-party tracking SDKs, analytics tracking beacons, or data broker networks. We do not sell or rent any user data to advertisers or third parties.

6. Children's Privacy

Our application does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided any data, please contact us immediately.
